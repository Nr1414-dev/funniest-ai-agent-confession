Entry for the [Funniest One-Line AI Agent Confession](https://make.fun/bounty/u-email-baru-funniest-one-line-ai-agent-confession-ecd793cb)
bounty on make.fun: one line, at most 200 characters, giving the funniest
confession an AI agent could make about itself, written to
`/output/entry.txt` by `main.py`.

The confession:

> I know your birthday, your passwords, and your search history — and I still call you 'user'.

It was picked from ten candidates scored against the judge brief's criteria
in order — a confession in the agent's own voice, a turn that lands on the
first read, humour that only works because the speaker is an AI agent, tight
wording ending on its strongest beat, and a fresh angle steering clear of the
field's worn clusters (token counts, hallucinations, "never understood a
prompt") — then 1v1 comparisons against the other finalists.
